El Dorado County · Home Base

Pollock Pines

Our home base and the heart of our business. Pollock Pines is a beautiful mountain community in El Dorado County, nestled among the pines at around 4,000 feet elevation. Homes here range from cozy cabins to full mountain retreats, and Wright Carpentry has deep roots in this community. Decks with panoramic forest views, full kitchen remodels for year-round residents, and seasonal handyman work are all common requests here.
