Ian Wright: Local Craftsman, Local Values

Handcrafted work for neighbors who deserve the best

From Foothills to Master Craftsman

Ian Wright grew up in the El Dorado County foothills, learning the value of honest work from an early age. After decades of hands-on experience in residential and custom remodeling, he founded Wright Carpentry with one simple goal: to bring master-level craftsmanship to every neighbor he works with.

He doesn't believe in shortcuts, cut corners, or "good enough." Every project—whether a full kitchen remodel or a simple handyman repair—gets the same attention to detail and commitment to quality that made his reputation in the community.

When Ian takes on a project, you're getting the work of someone who grew up understanding what it means to build something that lasts. He lives in Pollock Pines with his family, serves the same communities he knows best, and takes pride in showing up, doing the work right, and being the kind of contractor his neighbors can rely on.

Roots Run Deep in El Dorado County

Ian didn't move to Pollock Pines to start a business. He came home.

Growing up in the foothills taught him what it means to be part of a real community—where your reputation is everything, where neighbors become friends, and where doing the job right isn't just a business practice, it's a way of life.

That's why Wright Carpentry operates differently than out-of-area contractors or large firms. We're here because we live here. We care about the quality of homes and lives in our communities, and we show up the same way for every client—whether it's a $5,000 repair or a $50,000 remodel.

When you hire Ian, you're hiring someone who has invested his life in these foothills, who knows the local character of your home and community, and who will be here long after the project is finished.
