Fence Installation & Repair

Whether you need a new fence for privacy, security, pets, or property boundaries — or you have an existing fence that's leaning, rotting, or coming apart — Wright Carpentry handles it. We work with wood, cedar, and other materials that hold up well in the local climate.

We dig posts to proper depth, use concrete footings where required, and build fences that don't lean after the first winter. Ian also repairs existing fences — replacing individual posts, panels, and gates without requiring a full replacement when the structure is still sound.
