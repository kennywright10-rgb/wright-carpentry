Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do full home builds or major additions?+
Wright Carpentry specializes in repair, restoration, and trade-level carpentry work. We're not taking on full home builds or large structural additions at this time.
How long does a free estimate take?+
Most on-site estimates take 20-45 minutes depending on the scope of work. Ian will walk the job with you, ask questions, and follow up with a written estimate within 1-2 business days.
What areas do you serve?+
We primarily serve Pollock Pines, Sierra Springs, Camino, Cedar Grove, Placerville, El Dorado Hills, Cameron Park, and Folsom.
Do you handle permits?+
For projects that require permits, Ian will advise you on what's needed and can assist in coordinating the permitting process with El Dorado County.
How do I pay?+
Wright Carpentry accepts check, cash, and most major credit cards. For larger projects, we work out a milestone-based payment schedule.
