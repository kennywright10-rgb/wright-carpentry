Meet Your Contractor

Ian Wright — Built on Skill, Driven by Pride

Ian Wright grew up with tools in his hands and a passion for building things right. Based in Pollock Pines in the heart of El Dorado County, Ian started Wright Carpentry to bring honest, high-quality construction services to his community.

Unlike big contractors who outsource everything, Ian personally oversees every project — from the initial walkthrough to the final nail. His clients know exactly who they're dealing with: a craftsman who takes pride in every measurement, cut, and finish.

Wright Carpentry specializes in remodeling, room additions, decks, retaining walls, and the full range of handyman services that keep homes looking and functioning their best. While full new-home builds aren't in the lineup yet, there's almost nothing Ian can't tackle when it comes to making your existing home better.
