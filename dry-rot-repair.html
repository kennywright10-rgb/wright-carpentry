Dry Rot Repair

Dry rot is one of those problems that gets much worse — and much more expensive — the longer it goes unaddressed. Moisture finds its way into wood framing, siding, window sills, soffits, eaves, and subfloors, and the fungal decay spreads from there.

Ian finds the source of the moisture first, removes all compromised wood, treats the area, and replaces everything with properly sealed, durable materials. We don't just patch over rot — we eliminate the cause so it doesn't come back in the same spot. Sierra foothill homes are especially susceptible during wet winters; early intervention saves thousands.

✓ Moisture source identification
✓ Siding, soffits, and fascia repair
✓ Window and door sill replacement
✓ Subfloor and structural framing repair
✓ Deck and exterior wood restoration
✓ Treatment and sealing to prevent recurrence
