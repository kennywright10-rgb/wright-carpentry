Door & Window Installation

Doors that stick, drag, won't latch, or have drafty gaps are a daily frustration and an energy efficiency problem. Wright Carpentry installs and repairs interior and exterior doors — from entry doors and patio doors to interior passage doors and closet doors.

Proper door installation requires precision. Ian ensures doors are level, plumb, and properly sealed, with hardware that works smoothly and stays that way. We also handle window installation and trim — replacing drafty single-pane windows with properly fitted, weather-sealed units and matching interior and exterior trim.
