Quick Answers

How quickly will Ian respond?+
Most inquiries get a call or text back within a few hours during business hours. You won't be waiting days to hear back.
Is the estimate really free?+
100% free, no strings attached. Ian visits your property, walks the job with you, and provides a written estimate. You decide what to do from there.
What if I'm not sure what service I need?+
That's fine — just describe the problem in the message box. Ian has seen a lot and can usually identify the right course of action once he sees the project in person.
Do you work on weekends?+
Saturday estimates and project work are available. Sunday is generally off, but reach out and Ian will let you know based on his current schedule.
